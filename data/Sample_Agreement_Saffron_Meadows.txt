AGREEMENT FOR SALE - SAMPLE DOCUMENT (FICTIONAL) - Saffron Meadows, Whitefield, Bengaluru
All names, numbers and facts are fictional. Some clauses are deliberately unfair for testing.

This Agreement is made at Bengaluru on 20 February 2026 between: (1) Priyadarshini Habitats Pvt. Ltd. (fictional), represented by Mr. R. Sample (the Promoter); and (2) Mr. Arjun Rao and Ms. Meera Rao (fictional) (the Allottees).

1. THE PROJECT AND REGISTRATION
Clause 1.1: The Promoter is developing a residential project called "Saffron Meadows" at Whitefield, Bengaluru Urban District (the "Project"). The Allottees have agreed to buy Flat No. 804 on the 8th floor of Tower B, with carpet area of 880 sq ft, super built-up area of 1,350 sq ft and one covered car park (the "Flat").
Clause 1.2: The Promoter has applied to the Karnataka Real Estate Regulatory Authority for registration of the Project. The registration number will be given to the Allottees once it is granted. Until then the Allottees agree to go ahead with this Agreement and will not hold the Promoter responsible for any delay in registration.

2. PRICE AND PAYMENT
Clause 2.1: The Total Price of the Flat is Rs. 92,00,000 (Rupees Ninety-Two Lakh only), calculated on the super built-up area of 1,350 sq ft.
Clause 2.2: The Allottees have already paid Rs. 18,40,000 (20% of the Total Price) as booking amount on 12 January 2026, before the signing of this Agreement. The balance shall be paid as per the Payment Plan in Schedule C.
Clause 2.3: Goods and Services Tax and any other taxes applicable to the sale shall be payable by the Allottees as and when due.
Clause 2.4: If the Allottees delay any payment, they shall pay interest at 24% per annum, compounded monthly. The Promoter shall not be liable to pay any interest to the Allottees for any delay on its part.

3. AREA OF THE FLAT
Clause 3.1: The areas stated in this Agreement are approximate. The final area may differ by up to 10% either way. Any increase shall be charged at the same rate and paid before possession. Any reduction shall be adjusted, without interest, after completion of the Project.

4. PLANS AND SPECIFICATIONS
Clause 4.1: The Promoter may, at its sole discretion and without the consent of the Allottees, modify the sanctioned plans, layout, specifications, fittings, amenities, common areas and building elevation, including the height or structure of any building. The Allottees shall not raise any objection.

5. POSSESSION
Clause 5.1: The Promoter proposes to hand over possession of the Flat by 31 December 2027 (the "Tentative Date"). This date is only an estimate.
Clause 5.2: The Promoter may extend the Tentative Date by up to 24 months, at its discretion, for any reason including shortage of labour or materials, market conditions, delay in government approvals or difficulty in raising finance.
Clause 5.3: The Allottees shall not be entitled to any interest, compensation or refund for any delay in possession, and waive all rights under any law in this regard.
Clause 5.4: The Allottees shall take possession within two months of the occupancy certificate being issued. If they do not, they shall pay maintenance charges from the end of that period.

6. CANCELLATION AND REFUND
Clause 6.1: If the Allottees miss any instalment by even one day, the Promoter may cancel the allotment without notice and forfeit 25% of the Total Price.
Clause 6.2: If the Project cannot be carried out because of a natural calamity such as flood, earthquake or cyclone, the Promoter shall refund the entire amount received within sixty days.

7. DEFECTS
Clause 7.1: The Promoter shall repair structural defects only, if they are reported within one year from the date of the occupancy certificate. After that period the Promoter shall have no liability for any defect.

8. DISPUTES
Clause 8.1: All disputes shall be settled only by a sole arbitrator appointed by the Promoter, with the seat of arbitration at Hyderabad. The Allottees waive their right to approach the Real Estate Regulatory Authority or any other forum.

SCHEDULE C - PAYMENT PLAN (share of Total Price Rs. 92,00,000)
- Booking amount (already paid): 20% (Rs. 18,40,000)
- Within 15 days of this Agreement: 10% (Rs. 9,20,000)
- On completion of foundation: 15% (Rs. 13,80,000)
- On completion of slabs: 25% (Rs. 23,00,000)
- On completion of walls: 15% (Rs. 13,80,000)
- On completion of flooring: 10% (Rs. 9,20,000)
- At possession: 5% (Rs. 4,60,000)
- Total: 100% (Rs. 92,00,000)
